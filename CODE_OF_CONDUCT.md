# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project free from harassment and
welcoming regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and repair harm.
- Protect private information and security reports.
- Prioritize the health of the community over individual preference.

Harassment, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, threats, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation for behavior they judge harmful. Report urgent private
concerns through the repository's private vulnerability-reporting channel and
mark the report as a conduct matter. Reports will be handled confidentially to
the extent practical. Enforcement decisions may be appealed to a maintainer who
was not involved in the original decision when one is available.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
